Following the paper kite
Two bicycles are racing downhill
Through blooming flowers
Leaving sleepy yards
April morning
Fluttering ribbons in the hair
Undone jackets
Flying in the wind like sails
Bicycles left behind
Sneakers hastily shuck off into the wet grass
Racing through the slopes
Down to the marine cool azure
Balancing on the narrow parapet
Running towards the edge of the Earth
Feeding white seagulls
Counting ships on the horizon
Being terribly unserious
Happy and quite silly
But to see everything much brighter
Feel everything hundreds of times stronger
To intoxicating wind
And to salty splashes exposing your face
To laugh, to spin
To spread your arms and fall into the sand
How frivolously and generously
Is gifting its promises the spring
Bodes so many bright days
But this one won't return to us
Surrendering to overwhelming feelings
Falling silent hand in hand
Watching with aching sadness
The burning out sunset

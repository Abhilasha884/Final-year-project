I heard your voice in a dream
With lucid clarity
These few words you spoke to me
Take care of yourself, and your family
Honestly
Your voice clear as light
Across the night of time
And still my friend you show to me
Compassion and honesty
Oh, my friend where are you now?
Cradled on eternal bough?
Have you crossed over?
Or has your soul been caught?
Or have you discovered all dimensions of God?
Your prayer to God was heard with a cost
Your Mind, your life
My friend I lost
So you forsake the Holy Ghost
For the key to the cosmos

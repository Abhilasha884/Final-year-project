Well, they say there is an isle, an ocean away,
where time doesn´t care if dreaming can sway.
Plenty o´food and plenty o´wine,
people dancing, ain´t that fine?
A sure way to go to islands so free,
you close both your eyes, believe what you see.
You´ll be there in no time flat.
Baby, this ist where it´s at.

The winds, they are so warm, you wear just a smile.
You eat and you love and laugh all the while.
But beware the happiness!
You will get lost in loneliness.
The mist from the sea will hide you away.
The game that you play will turn night to day.
You´ll be gone for sure, my friend!
What a foxy way to end!
